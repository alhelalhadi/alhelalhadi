## How I work

**The brief comes first.** No net-new build starts without a requirements handshake. Most briefs arrive with the actual problem missing, and finding it is cheaper before the pixels than after.

**Real content, always.** Placeholder copy hides design failures until they are expensive to fix.

**Decisions get written down.** A decision that only exists in a chat thread is not locked. Every project carries a decision log, so the reasoning survives past the last revision.

**Market context before market work.** I do not design for an audience I have not written a context block for. Assumptions about users are the most expensive kind.

**AI accelerates execution, not judgment.** Research, IA drafts, and first-pass exploration move faster with it. The direction, the critique, and the final call are mine, and I can defend every one of them.
